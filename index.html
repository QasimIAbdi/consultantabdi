■

We guide international organizations and corporations to access funding opportunities from Norway.

Through our network and expertise, we help partners connect with:

  • Norwegian Government Pension Fund Global (Oil Fund) – the world’s largest sovereign wealth fund

  • Innovation Norway – grants and support for international business development and green projects

  • Enova SF – funding for energy innovation, low-carbon technologies, and climate solutions

  • Norad (Norwegian Agency for Development Cooperation) – support for sustainable development and energy access projects

  • Norfund – investments in renewable energy and sustainable infrastructure in emerging markets

  • European and EEA-linked programs (Horizon Europe, EUREKA) – research and innovation funding accessible through Norway’s membership

Our role is to bridge opportunities between Norway and the world, helping you unlock resources for growth, sustainability, and global impact.

What We Do

Strategy & Deal Origination

We design cross-border structures that match Norwegian capital with credible local partners.

At Qasim Consult Corporation, we specialize in identifying opportunities where Norwegian funding instruments—such as the Oil Fund, Innovation Norway, Enova, Norad, and Norfund—can be connected with international projects.

Our role is to structure partnerships that meet the strict standards of transparency, governance, and sustainability expected by Norwegian institutions. By combining local market insight with Norwegian capital and expertise, we unlock strategies that create long-term value for all sides.

Whether it is energy, infrastructure, or development finance, we guide organizations from the first idea through deal origination, ensuring that projects are attractive to investors and aligned with Norway’s global priorities.

⤷ View: Book a Strategy Call

Government & Embassy Relations

We open doors to commercial/energy attachés and ministries for formal introductions.

Building international partnerships often requires access to decision-makers at the highest levels. At Qasim Consult Corporation, we help organizations navigate the diplomatic and governmental landscape by facilitating introductions to embassies, commercial attachés, and relevant ministries.

Our experience ensures that your projects are presented in the right format, to the right people, at the right time. By bridging your initiatives with official channels, we increase credibility and accelerate the path to collaboration.

Whether your goal is to secure government funding, attract investment from Norway’s Oil Fund, or align with national energy and innovation priorities, we provide the formal introductions and strategic guidance needed to open those doors.

Grants &
Applications

End-to-end grant support: eligibility checks, drafting, budgets, and compliance.

Securing Norwegian grants can be complex — from eligibility requirements to application drafting, budget structuring, and compliance. At Qasim Consult Corporation, we provide complete guidance to ensure your project meets the highest standards.

We help international organizations and governments navigate opportunities with Innovation Norway, Norad, Enova, and Norfund, making sure applications align with Norway’s sustainability, governance, and impact criteria.

Our role is to simplify the process, reduce risk of rejection, and increase the likelihood of funding approval. By combining technical expertise with strategic positioning, we give your projects the clarity and credibility needed to succeed.

Our Founder

■

Qasim Ilyas Abdi

Founder of Consultant Qasim Corporation, a company dedicated to building bridges between Norway and the world. With a proven background as a Senior Engineer and team leader in the Norwegian public sector, Qasim has led projects in data engineering, analytics platforms, and digital transformation, giving him deep insight into how Norway manages information, technology, and strategic development.

Building on this experience, he now specializes in helping organizations and governments unlock access to Norwegian funding instruments, including the Government Pension Fund Global (Oil Fund), Innovation Norway, Enova, Norad, and Norfund.

He is passionate about creating cross-border partnerships that combine Norwegian expertise in energy, sustainability, and governance with opportunities in emerging markets. By aligning local strengths with Norwegian capital and technology, Qasim’s mission is to design strategies that deliver mutual growth, long-term value, and global impact.
